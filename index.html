Pebble Wars 11 September 2026

11-09-26 UI Improvements

A pass on the game's book-styled windows: the NPC dialogue book got small readability fixes, and the player's Character/Skills/Quests/Settings windows were merged into a single book that matches its look exactly.

NPC Dialogue Book

The book's bookmark ("×") sits at the top, tucked into the page like a real bookmark, instead of floating as a plain round button.

Turning in a quest now shows just the flavor text and a "Complete Quest".

The Player Book

Character, Skills, Quests, and Settings used to be separate windows with inconsistent styling. They're now four bookmarked tabs of one shared boo.,

The Character tab arranges the equipment slots as a proper paper doll (Head alone, then Cape/Chest, Legs/Belt, and Main/Off Hand paired up) on the left page. The right page toggles between Stats and a Viewer for the portrait, instead of cramming both onto one crowded page.

Skills keeps its two-page layout - disciplines and training progress on the left, learned abilities on the right - now reskinned in the same parchment look as everything else instead of its old dark-green panel.

The old Escape-key popup is gone - Settings is just another tab now, reachable the same way as the others.
